#Play Football Draft
A 2-player football draft and auction game.

##Play
https://kushalbs07.github.io/playfootballdraft/

##Features

- 2-player football draft
- $20 starting budget
- Live player bidding
- 5 starters + 2 substitutes
- Formation and lineup management
- Player chemistry and scoring
- VS AI
- Private matches
- Random matchmaking
